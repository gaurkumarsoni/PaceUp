The short version

PaceUp does not collect your personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your own computer.

What PaceUp stores, and where

PaceUp stores your timer settings, tasks, goals, journal entries, notes, alarms and a log of the steps you finish. This is saved in a file on your PC. You can open it, back it up, move it to another folder, or delete it. The developer cannot see it and never receives it.

What PaceUp sends over the internet

PaceUp does not send your data anywhere. It has no server, and it does not connect to one to run.

  • When you click a web link inside a note, your default browser opens that link. That is the only time PaceUp involves the internet.
  • If you type in a note or journal entry, the app's built-in web engine may download a spelling dictionary file from Google's servers the first time spell checking is used. No text you write is sent.
  • If you choose to keep your data folder inside a service such as OneDrive or Google Drive, that service syncs the file under its own terms and privacy policy. PaceUp does not do this for you.

Exporting and importing

When you export to Excel, CSV or a backup file, the file is saved where you choose, on your computer. When you import, PaceUp reads the file you select. Nothing is uploaded.

Notifications and alarms

Timer and alarm notifications are created on your PC. They are not sent from a server.

Children

PaceUp does not ask for any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same web address with a new date.
